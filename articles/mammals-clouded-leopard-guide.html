Clouded Leopard: Identification, Habitat and Natural History

An introductory Animal World guide to Clouded Leopard, with practical pointers for learning about this animal and checking reliable species information.

Classification and identity

Clouded Leopard is grouped under Mammals, in the directory section “Big cats”. Common names can be used differently in different regions. Check a recognised taxonomic database for its accepted scientific name, synonyms and current classification.

Look at a combination of body structure, size range, markings, proportions, age-related changes and geographic location. Similar animals may share visible features, so avoid identifying an unfamiliar animal from colour alone. A local field guide or expert observation can help confirm identification.

Diet and feeding ecology

Diet depends on anatomy, behaviour and the food available in each habitat. Feeding relationships connect animals with other organisms and help shape ecosystems.
