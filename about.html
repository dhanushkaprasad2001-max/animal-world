About Animal World

Animal World is an educational website created for people who are curious about the animals that share our planet. Our goal is to make animal information approachable, organised and useful for readers of different ages.

What you can explore

Our guides introduce wild animals, companion animals, birds, marine life, reptiles and insects. Depending on the species, a guide may cover its scientific name, habitat, diet, physical features, behaviour, reproduction, care considerations and conservation challenges.

Our approach

We aim to use clear language and responsible, evidence-informed summaries. Animal facts can vary between populations, regions and individuals, so readers should treat general figures as approximations rather than universal rules.

Wildlife respect

Wild animals belong in their natural habitats. Please observe wildlife from a safe distance and never handle, feed or disturb an animal unless qualified local professionals advise that it is appropriate.

Thank you for exploring the animal kingdom with us.
